## Observations

* **O-1 IOC-type auctions vs indexer lag.** Market, close and streaming orders (lives of 200 / 300 DAA) were killed unfilled 6
  times out of 7 (the KAS market buy filled), and a pair FOK with `--life 600daa` too; the KAS IOC and FOK with `--life 600daa` filled. Both matchers plan from
  their indexer, 60-110 s behind the node in this soak. Known (README "IOC and indexer lag"); `kob place market|close|streaming`
  has no `--life`.
* **O-2 crossed KAS books.** Sampling the top of book every 20 s for 30 min: crossed in 40-70 % of samples (p50 24 bps, runs
  up to 4-6 min; the longest was the test's own DCA, throttled by its interval). Timelines show these crossings being
  filled in chunks about every 950 DAA (one indexer-lag cycle per fill round, e.g. `1146c680...` filled at 595801317 and
  595802262), so it is the lag cadence plus the weighted sampling, not a matcher stall. No pre-roll baseline was sampled.
* **O-3 `/swap` order conflicts.** About half of the bots' `/swap` payments fail `order_conflict` before and after the roll
  (the quoted market-maker bid is amended within the indexer lag).
* **O-4 `kob cancel` with several ids** submits one transaction per order (the web's cancel-all batches up to 6).
