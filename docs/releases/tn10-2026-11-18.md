# TN10 upgrade, 2026-11-18 00:00 UTC

## Before you upgrade

- Orders placed before the switch can only be cancelled afterwards. Cancel them or let them fill first.
- Bots and scripts: update the `kob` CLI from master.

## New

- **Bought tokens.** A buy order can deliver each fill separately (default), collect fills and send them at the end, or add them to one UTXO.
- **Claim.** Take what a collecting buy order bought: Claim, Claim and merge, or Claim and sell, in one approval.
- **Curve sell.** One sell order whose price steps up as it sells. Create token can now keep a share in your wallet and sell the rest at one price or on a curve. A ladder can be placed as one curve order.
- **Fill now.** A limit or FOK order that crosses the book fills directly from your wallet at the resting price, with no executor. Market and IOC still go to executors.
- **Executor tip.** An optional switch under Advanced.
- **UI.**
  - Progress steps for multi-signature flows.
  - Red toasts for every failure.
  - A faster Portfolio.
  - A shorter Merge all button.
  - Aligned Balances columns.
  - A mid line on the chart.

## Changed

- **Security.** Contract fixes from internal reviews (DLV-07, H1). The indexer checks the chain itself (PoW and merkle roots).
- **CLI.**
  - New: `kob claim`, `kob place curve`, `kob sweep`, `--delivery`, and a fee ceiling (`--allow-high-fee`).
  - Removed: old spellings, `kob order`, and the 8x8 issuance.
- **Tools.** [kob-mcp](../../tools/kob-mcp/README.md) for AI agents, and [mm-bot](../../tools/mm-bot/README.md) for market making.
- **Fees.** Pair fills cost about 0.00002 KAS more.
